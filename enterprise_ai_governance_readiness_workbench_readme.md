## 1. Business Problem & Solution Delivered

**The Challenge:** EuroFin Services S.A. lacked a consistent process to track its 15 AI systems, assess EU AI Act/GDPR applicability, collect audit evidence, and assign clear accountability for remediation.

| Business Challenge | Governance Workbench Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| Fragmented AI system information | Centralised AI system inventory |
| Unclear regulatory applicability | Preliminary EU AI Act & GDPR/DPIA screening workflow |
| Inconsistent risk & vendor evaluation | Structured risk, security, and supplier assessment |
| Missing supporting documentation | Centralised evidence and documentation tracker |
| Unclear stakeholder accountability | Assigned system owners and specialist human reviewers |
| Limited executive visibility | Management governance dashboard and status tracking |
| Untracked remediation actions | Integrated risk, issue, and action registers |

*Disclaimer: This tool is a governance decision-support prototype and does not constitute a formal legal compliance determination or certification.*

## 2. Demonstrated AI Implementation PM Capabilities

As **AI Implementation Project Manager**, I managed the simulated delivery lifecycle from initiation through operational handover:

* **Initiation & Planning:** Project Charter, scope definition, Work Breakdown Structure (WBS), 38-day schedule, and Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM).
* **Governance & Stakeholder Alignment:** RACI matrix, RAID management, and human-oversight approval gates.
* **Financial & Agile Control:** Budget baseline (€53,590), Earned Value Management (EVM) tracking (€5,040 positive variance), capacity planning, and sprint backlog prioritisation.
* **Quality, Risk & Handover:** Test register, defect tracking, UAT preparation, residual risk register, operational handover plan, benefits realisation plan, and closure report.

### Delivery Lifecycle Summary

| Phase | Key Activities | Core Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Initiation** | Business case, objectives, governance setup | Project Charter |
| **2. Discovery** | Stakeholder analysis, AI system discovery | AI System Register, RTM |
| **3. Planning** | Scope, schedule, capacity, cost baseline | WBS, RAID Log, RACI, Budget |
| **4. Design** | Governance workflow & oversight controls | Solution Design |
| **5. Build** | AI prototype & workflow configuration | Working Governance Workbench |
| **6. Validation** | Test execution, defect triage, UAT readiness | Test Register, Issues/Defects Log |
| **7. Handover** | Support model, training & operational readiness | Operational Handover Package |
| **8. Closure** | Financial reconciliation & lessons learned | Closure Report, Benefits Plan |
